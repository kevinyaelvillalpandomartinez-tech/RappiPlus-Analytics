# E-commerce Customer Journey & Conversion Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes real-world behavioral data from an online cosmetics store to understand how customers interact with the e-commerce purchase journey.

The dataset contains customer events such as product views, cart additions, cart removals, and purchases, allowing the analysis of user progression through the conversion funnel.

The project focuses on identifying funnel drop-off points, measuring conversion performance, analyzing product and brand purchasing behavior, evaluating repeat purchases, and understanding customer retention through cohort analysis.

The objective is to transform large-scale behavioral and transactional data into actionable insights that can support decisions related to customer experience, conversion, product performance, and retention.

## Business Questions

The project will explore questions such as:

- How do users progress from product views to cart additions and purchases?
- What is the overall conversion rate from product view to purchase?
- At which stage of the customer journey does the highest drop-off occur?
- Which products, categories, and brands demonstrate stronger purchasing and conversion performance?
- Which products and brands generate the highest purchase revenue?
- What percentage of purchasing customers make repeat purchases?
- How does customer retention change after a customer's first purchase?
- Which customer cohorts demonstrate stronger retention behavior?
